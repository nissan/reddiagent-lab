## Why two licenses

ReddiAgent's goal is for ADL (the Agent Definition Language) and its companion
specs to become community-maintained open specifications. CC BY 4.0 lets anyone
republish, translate, teach from, and build on the spec text. Apache 2.0 gives
implementers a patent grant and a familiar, permissive license for the
reference tooling.
